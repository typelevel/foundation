# Board of Directors Meeting Minutes

The meeting was called by the board during the meeting on January 30, for Friday, March 6, 2026 at 1:00 PM PT.

## Attendance

* Arman Bilge (President, Executive Director)
* Andrew Valencik (Secretary)
* Mark Waks (Treasurer)

## Insurance

We reviewed quotes for workers' compensation insurance, general liability insurance, and Directors and Officers insurance. We decided to proceed with workers' compensation and general liability coverage and defer Directors and Officers coverage until our financial position is clearer.

#### Motion
We move to purchase workers' compensation and general liability insurance coverage.

_Passed unanimously._

## Payroll

#### Motion
We move to contract with ADP for payroll services and put Arman on payroll.

_Passed unanimously._

## Open source leadership training

We discussed the course ["Leadership Training for Open Source"][training] by Osio Labs. We decided to investigate further before committing.

[training]: https://community.osiolabs.com/pages/leadership-training

## Financial transparency

We noted that the new Open Collective page does not automatically sync all transactions or reflect our current balance. We considered entering expenses manually and deferred a decision.

## AI policy

We agreed that Typelevel needs an AI policy and that the Technical Steering Committee (TSC) should lead its development with community input.

#### Motion
We task the TSC to work with the community to draft an AI policy.

_Passed unanimously._

## Fundraising

We discussed pending sponsorships and fundraising efforts in an executive session.

## Executive Director priorities

We identified fundraising and Cats Effect v3.7.0 as priorities. Arman noted that Google Summer of Code was requiring a lot of attention so we decided to set an earlier deadline for applications.
