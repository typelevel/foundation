# Board of Directors Meeting Minutes

The meeting was called by the board during the meeting on October 24, for Friday, November 7, 2025 at 12:00 PM PT.

## Attendance

* Arman Bilge (President, Executive Director)
* Daniel Spiewak
* Andrew Valencik (Secretary)
* Mark Waks (Treasurer)

## Bylaw amendments

[Proposed amendments][pr1] to the bylaws were merged, following unanimous written approval, and the `typelevel/foundation` repository was made public.

## Conflict of Interest agreements

We acknowledged that the bylaws require us to annually sign a statement regarding conflicts of interest, and discussed the mechanics of how we will do this.

## Assets

We discussed what assets belong to Typelevel. This veered into a discussion of intellectual property in open source. We agreed that we should excercise due diligence, investigate the current best practices, and try to follow them.

## Insurance

Arman shared that we were quoted $1,255 for 12 months of Directors and Officers insurance. We felt that this quote is reasonable and affordable and that we are inclined to purchase it. We deferred making a final decision until we get a second quote.

## State charity registration

Arman shared that state charity registration compliance may cost us $2k–$7k annually. We felt this is a non-trivial cost and want to look into this more before making a decision.

## Fundraising

We discussed pending sponsorships and fundraising efforts in an executive session.

## Executive Director employment

We confirmed our intention to employ Arman as an independent contractor. Arman will follow-up with Open Source Collective to finalize the details.

## Strategic Plan

We briefly broached the subject of a strategic plan, but recognized it is an enormous topic. We considered the question, "what does it mean to be a Typelevel project?" and noted the existence of libraries in a "weird middle ground" between affiliate and organization, such as FS2 and http4s. Ultimately we need to discuss this with the technical steering committee.

## Next board meeting

We scheduled the next board meeting for Friday, December 5, 2025 at 1:00 PM PT.
