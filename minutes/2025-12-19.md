## Open Source Collective Memorandum of Understanding

Arman reported that Open Source Collective currently views the Typelevel Foundation as a "commercial maintainer" of the Typelevel project. To reimburse the Foundation's expenses from our hosted funds, we will need to sign a Memorandum of Understanding to agree that we will only spend those funds to further their exempt purpose.
