### Technical Steering Committee

We discussed the role of the Technical Steering Committee (TSC) and the related topic of what does it mean to be a Typelevel project. We agreed that becoming an organization project does not mean that the TSC usurps ownership of the code, but rather, Typelevel backstops maintenance of the project, by controlling the group id. This is not the case for affiliate projects, which the TSC may designate so that a project relevant to our ecosystem can be promoted on our website. We acknowleded the existence of "in-between" projects such as http4s or Circe that we consider "core" to the Typlevel stack, but are not hosted on our infrastructure.

We clarified the distinction in enforcement in the case that a maintainer violates the Code of Conduct (CoC). If they are a maintainer of an affiliate project, then the project is unaffiliated, but if they are the maintainer of an organization project, then they are removed from the project, but the project remains with Typelevel.

We suggested that the TSC and CoC committees should work together.
