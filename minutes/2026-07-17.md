## Accountable Plan

#### Motion
We move to establish an Accountable Plan for reimbursing staff and volunteers for out-of-pocket expenses. Expenses must be directly connected to the business of the Foundation. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days of paying or incurring the expense and provide adequate substantiation, including the business purpose. Any excess advances must be returned within 120 days.

_Passed unanimously._
